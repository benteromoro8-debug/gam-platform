GAM Community Guidelines
1. Respect every member.
2. Do not share passwords or private information.
3. Do not harass other members.
4. Share educational and appropriate resources.
5. Check that links are trustworthy before opening them.
6. Never publish another person's private files or information.

Resource privacy: GAM shares the links you post, not the original files. Make sure you have permission to share a resource. Only use a public or appropriately shared link when other GAM members need to access it.
