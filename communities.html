🤝 What happens inside a community?

GAM communities are designed around people, not just lessons. Members can ask questions, discuss ideas, learn from one another and join sessions organized by approved tutors and peer tutors.

As GAM grows, communities can develop smaller specialized groups and learning divisions based on experience and demonstrated performance.

💬 Discussions Ask questions and exchange ideas.
🧑‍🎓 Peer Learning Students can help students.
👨‍🏫 Tutors Approved tutors can organize sessions.
🏆 Progress Build experience and earn opportunities.
📅 Events Join live learning sessions.
🛡️ Safe Community GAM moderation protects members.
